# Response analyzer

A learner's reply in, a diagnosis out: whether the intent was missed, and what accounts for it.

The engine calls it at the outer turn and again inside the tangent, with the same contract both times — the target differs, the judgement does not. A reply judged against what the agent asked or stated; an attempt judged against what the agent has just asked again.

It diagnoses and nothing else. It does not correct, explain, or write.
