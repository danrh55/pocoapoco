# Purpose

Pocoapoco is a comprehension companion for real Spanish content. The learner
pastes a YouTube video they want to understand, and the app negotiates its
vocabulary with them — questions in Spanish, answered in English — until the
video's words form a dense mental model instead of a pile of unrelated ones.

The claim it rests on: *negotiated exposure to a video's vocabulary makes that
video comprehensible on rewatch.* The rewatch is the point — the retrieval, the
spacing, and the felt reward.

## The problem it answers

Comprehensible input works, but pure input is throttled twice:

- **Frequency** — any given word recurs too rarely to stick.
- **Structure** — exposure is a random walk, so words accumulate as isolated
  items, never a connected web.

Dreaming Spanish and its kin are *broad* input: maximum variety, minimum
density. Pocoapoco is the density layer on top of content the learner already
wants — *narrow* input, on demand, dialed to one learner and one video. It is a
complement, not a competitor.

## End state

The learner comprehends real Spanish content fluently. Generation is deferred,
not abandoned: the dense mental lexicon built by comprehension is the on-ramp
to producing it.

## The mechanism

The learner's **signal of non-understanding is the hinge.** One signal does
three jobs at once:

- it is the **interaction** trigger — input is modified in response to it;
- it is the **noticing** trigger — attention is directed at a form;
- it is the **density** trigger — the system re-exposes that form across
  varied contexts.

The conversation is a loop of comprehension questions. A question is an
**anchor**: it supplies the shared object — the video — and a reason to keep
talking. The learner answers in English (crosstalk). A wrong answer is the
signal; the reply is *input modification, never explanation*.

## Who this is for

Open. The comprehension-first turn widens the audience past the intermediate
producer toward anyone who can follow pitched-down Spanish and wants to
understand real content. Beginner-to-conversational is the working assumption,
not a settled claim.

## Theory

- **Input hypothesis** — acquisition runs on comprehensible input, i+1.
- **Interaction hypothesis** — input is modified in response to a signal that
  it wasn't understood.
- **Noticing hypothesis** — some active attention to form is required;
  comprehension alone is not enough.
- **Narrow input** — staying within one topic makes vocabulary recur, dense and
  connected.
- **The mental lexicon as a network** — words are nodes, contexts are edges; a
  *dense* model means many edges within a cluster.

One twist this app claims: the video is fixed, so the input cannot be modified.
What gets modified is the *learner* — the negotiation builds the model that
makes the same fixed input comprehensible. Input modification, applied to the
mind instead of the message.

## Invariants

1. **The register is crosstalk.** The app speaks Spanish, pitched down; the
   learner answers in English. English is never translated back to them.
2. **Never explain.** A missed word or idea is re-exposed, restated, or
   reframed — never defined, never given a rule.
3. **Never feel like a test.** The mechanism is a comprehension check; the
   surface is a conversation. Anxiety blocks acquisition.
4. **The probe is internal.** The learner sees a natural question; the system
   sees the dimension it probes. As error types once were, probe dimensions are
   never named.

## Assumptions

1. Negotiated exposure to a video's vocabulary makes that video comprehensible
   on rewatch.
2. Comprehension of written Spanish transfers to generation.
3. A wrong answer is a faithful enough signal of non-understanding to steer the
   negotiation.
4. Spanish pitched down in question and reply stays worth returning for.
5. The learner answers honestly — they want to understand the video, not to
   beat the check.

## Open questions

- What "understood" means — which confusions the questions should probe.
- How the learner's level is known: a pre-modeled path, or the learner's own
  signals alone.
- What happens when the learner wanders or can't follow the partner.

## Not in this version

Generation (Spanish output) · speech · memory of the user across videos ·
persona and opinion · anything beyond the rewatch itself as retrieval.
