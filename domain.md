# Language learning

Pocoapoco helps people learning a new language build a dense, interconnected understanding of it by solving communication challenges. Learners encounter and work through problems that make the language meaningful, gradually expanding what they can understand and express.

## Learning progression

Learning begins with listening and comprehension. New learners may have little vocabulary and may not yet recognize the sounds of the language, so they need context and learning aids that make input understandable while keeping the vocabulary in play relevant to their interests.

As learners gain enough vocabulary and basic comprehension, they can begin generating sentences in written form. This gives them practice thinking in the language and building a richer model of what is being communicated, without pronunciation as an added burden. Learners need ways to notice errors and attempt corrections on the sentences they write.

Comprehension and generation are overlapping activities, not discrete levels. Generation fills gaps that input alone cannot cover. They feed into each other.

## Challenge and interaction

Communication challenges are the problems learners solve to work with the language. Conversations are the interface for those challenges. The app or agent layers cognitive load and tailors each challenge to the learner's current ability and interests.

## Scope

Pocoapoco aims to help learners reach the point where they know what they want to say and the only remaining challenge is pronounciation. Free-form speech and real-life conversations with other people are beyond the app's scope.
